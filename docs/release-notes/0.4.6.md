# Taskcode 0.4.6

The indicator now finds room on more taskbar layouts, including taskbars docked on the side.

## Placement

- **Widgets:** with the Widgets button on, the indicator stays visible and sits just before Widgets instead of disappearing.
- **Centered taskbar:** when the indicator does not fit next to the tray, it moves to the larger free space on the left at full size instead of shrinking into a scrolling strip. The space next to the tray is still used whenever the whole indicator fits there.
- **Drag to stick:** drag the indicator along the taskbar and release it on the left or right half to keep it on that side (top or bottom on a vertical taskbar). The choice is saved. A plain click still opens Details.

## Vertical taskbars

- When the taskbar is docked on the left or right, a vertical pill stacks each logo above its numbers.
- **Essential**, **Donuts** and **Color Strip** are available there. The longer styles are grayed out in the style list and show as Essential; your saved style comes back when the taskbar returns to the top or bottom.
- Details open beside the taskbar, next to the provider you point at.

Download **Taskcode-Setup-0.4.6-win-x64.exe** for installation or **Taskcode-0.4.6-win-x64.zip** for portable use. Existing users can update from **General > Check for updates**.

Windows 11 x64 and .NET Framework 4.8 are required. The installer remains unsigned; see the [installation guide](https://github.com/angelosc1/taskcode/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) for SmartScreen instructions.
