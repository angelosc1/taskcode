# Taskcode 0.4.4

The taskbar indicator now shows how much of each quota period has passed, and Claude quotas stay readable through a reset.

## Time marker

- Every indicator style can show elapsed period time, like the white arrow on the Details bars. Choose it in **Appearance > Time marker**:
  - **Arrows between bars** (default): with weekly quota on, the 5h arrow sits under its bar and the 7d arrow above its bar.
  - **Arrows outside bars**: each arrow sits on the outer side of its own bar.
  - **Line**: a thin line across the bar.
  - **Trail**: the part of the empty bar that time has already passed turns lighter.
  - **Off**.
- Donuts gain a small start mark at 12 o'clock, so the start and end of the period are clear at a glance.
- Essential and Sparkline have no bar, so they show the marker on a thin rule under the value.
- **Pace colors** (off by default) turn the marker green while usage stays within the elapsed time, and white once usage gets ahead.
- Your choice is saved and kept across updates.
- The **Details** button moved next to the Appearance heading.

## Claude quota

- Fixed a Claude quota showing "--" and "Unavailable" for up to five minutes right after it reset. At that moment the service can still report the retired window. Taskcode now shows the window as reset and checks again after 30 seconds.

Download **Taskcode-Setup-0.4.4-win-x64.exe** for installation or **Taskcode-0.4.4-win-x64.zip** for portable use. Existing users can update from **General > Check for updates**.

Windows 11 x64 and .NET Framework 4.8 are required. The installer remains unsigned; see the [installation guide](https://github.com/angelosc1/taskcode/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md) for SmartScreen instructions.
